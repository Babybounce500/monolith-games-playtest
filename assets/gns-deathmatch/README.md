# GNS Frag Arena local art

- `weapons/*.webp` are transparent, size-optimized copies of the ten original weapon renders in `NEW-GNS/`. GNS uses the laser carbine, particle beam, ion SMG, and siege gatling renders as its four first-person weapon views.
- `bots/*.webp` are transparent, size-optimized renders of ten distinct armored fighters. Each portrait shows its weapon raised and aimed in a ready-to-fire stance.
- These assets are scoped to the standalone GNS deathmatch. They are not registered in Sector Breach Siege.
